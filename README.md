## Vision

HorseCare is the oldest and best iOS equestrian app, with millions of community posts (Facebook for horses). We now deliver the Cal AI moment for every horse: snap a photo → instant weight, BCS, hay quality, conformation and hoof intelligence → clear daily actions.

**Core:** Daily management of *your* horse(s). Marketplace and Community amplify the flywheel.

## AI Possible vs Not

**Strong:** Weight/BCS from photo, hay type + approx protein, conformation, hoof visual, trends, risk flags.

**Limited:** Exact lab nutrition, medical diagnosis, absolute weight without refs, clinical lameness from stills.

Always disclose limits and confidence.

## The Gauntlet Flywheel

**Daily Loop (Cal AI + Duolingo + Strava):**
Trigger (notification/open) → Morning Briefing → Zero-friction Scan/Log → Instant AI reward + streak → Auto Plan → Investment (data + history)

**Amplifiers:** Shareable transformations → Community viral; Contextual Marketplace from alerts; Goal celebrations; Barn team contributions; Network effects on AI quality.
